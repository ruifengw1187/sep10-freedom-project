## Overview/Introduction/Context
Animation is the art of making static images look like they are moving. You draw a crazy amount of them and each has a slightly different image than the original image. Then you play them at an extremely fast rate. Usually 12 to 24 frames per second so your brain and eyes can seamlessly blend them in together. There are many types of animation like hand drawn 2D, 3D computer animation or stop-motion (Where you use real objects and move each object slightly to form movement) and motion graphics. Animators use tricks like the "12 Principles of Animation" and all that stuff to make the movements seem real and good. Animation has came a long way from hand drawn back in the days to today's CGI. But one thing still stands, make fired animation.🥶
